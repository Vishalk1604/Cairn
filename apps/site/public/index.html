Why “Cairn”

A cairn is a stack of stones left on a trail to mark the way.

Hikers build them for whoever comes next. A reminder is the same thing: a marker you leave for your future self. Cairn stacks yours, and keeps the next one in sight.

What it does

Everything with a date on it, in one place.

Type it the way you’d say it.

Write a line with a date in it and it becomes a task due at the right time, with no date picker. Add a #tag and it is filed too.

An inbox that reads itself.

Add your own free NVIDIA key and Cairn sorts new mail for you: what is important, which folder it belongs in, a one-line summary and a suggested task with its deadline. Mark a sender once and it remembers.

How it works

It keeps checking, even when your phone is off.

Cairn’s server looks at your mail and Classroom every ten minutes, sorts what is new and files it for both of your screens. You don’t keep anything open.

  1. 01

    Set it up on your phone

    Two minutes: sign in with Google, add your college account and choose what to watch.

  2. 02

    The server checks every ten minutes

    New email and Classroom posts are read and sorted, by AI if you added a key, by your own rules if not.

  3. 03

    It lands on both screens

    The same inbox and the same tasks on your phone and your laptop, with a notification for anything that needs you.

Privacy

Your mail stays yours.

Cairn reads, it never sends, changes or deletes anything. Here is what that means in practice.

Read the privacy policy →
  • Only a summary is kept

    A short summary of each new email or post is saved so both devices show the same inbox. Never the full text.

  • Your files, your Drive

    Photos and recordings live in your own Google Drive, in files only Cairn created.

  • AI only with your own key

    Mail is sent to an AI model only if you add your own NVIDIA key. Without one, simple rules sort it.

  • No ads, no analytics

    Nothing is sold or shared, and you can revoke Cairn’s access from your Google account at any time.

Questions

Good to know.

How do I get access?

Cairn is invite-only while it’s in beta. Message Vishal on LinkedIn with the Google account you’ll sign in with first, your personal one, and you’ll be added, usually within a day. Your college account doesn’t need its own invite: you add it from inside Cairn once you’re in.

Is Cairn really free?

Yes. There is no subscription and no ads. AI sorting uses your own free NVIDIA key, which you can add in setup or skip; without it Cairn sorts with simple rules.

Does it work with my college Google account?

Yes. Add it as a second account during setup and Cairn reads its Gmail and Classroom while your notes stay in your personal account. Some colleges block outside apps from Classroom; reading Classroom through its emails is in progress for that case.

Do I need to keep the app open?

No. Cairn’s server does the checking every ten minutes, so new coursework and important mail reach your phone as a notification even when the app is closed.

Why does Google warn me when I sign in?

Cairn is in an invite-only beta and hasn’t finished Google’s review yet. Until it has, Google shows a warning and limits Cairn to 100 people. Choose Advanced, then Go to Cairn.

Is there an iPhone or Mac version?

Not yet. Cairn runs on Android and Windows today.

What can Cairn do with my email?

Only read it. It never sends, changes or deletes anything, keeps a short summary rather than the full text, and you can remove its access at any time. The privacy policy has every detail.
