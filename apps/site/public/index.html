Cairn

Your inbox has deadlines in it. Cairn finds them.

A cairn is a stack of stones left on a trail to mark the way for whoever comes next. A reminder is the same thing: a marker you leave for your future self.

What it does

  • Notes and reminders that sync between a Windows desktop app and an Android phone.
  • Type submit DBMS assignment friday 6pm and get a correctly dated reminder — no date picker.
  • Optionally reads your Gmail and Google Classroom to turn assignment deadlines into reminders for you.

Status

Cairn is a personal project, built for and used by its author. It is not open for general sign-up, and there is nothing to download yet. The source is on GitHub.
