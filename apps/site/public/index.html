Cairn

Your inbox has deadlines in it. Cairn finds them.

A cairn is a stack of stones left on a trail to mark the way for whoever comes next. A reminder is the same thing: a marker you leave for your future self.

What it does

  • Notes and reminders that sync between a Windows desktop app and an Android phone.
  • Type submit DBMS assignment friday 6pm and get a correctly dated reminder — no date picker.
  • Optionally reads your Gmail and Google Classroom to turn assignment deadlines into reminders for you.
  • Your next deadline on your phone's home screen and pinned to your Windows desktop.

Download

Version 0.3 for Android 7.0 or newer and Windows 10 or 11. Both always point at the latest release.

Android Cairn-android.apk Windows Cairn-windows-setup.exe
Installing on Android
Open the APK from your browser's downloads. Android asks once whether your browser may install apps: tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, go back and tap Install. Updates install over the top and keep your notes; Cairn says in Settings when there is one.
Installing on Windows
Run the installer. It installs for your account only, no admin rights needed. It is not code-signed, so SmartScreen says it does not recognise the publisher: choose More info, then Run anyway. After that Cairn updates itself.
Signing in
Set Cairn up on your phone first, then sign in on the computer with the same Google account. Google warns that it has not verified Cairn, a small personal project; choose Advanced, then Go to Cairn.

Every release lists SHA-256 checksums on its release page, and the source is on GitHub.
