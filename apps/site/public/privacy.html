Privacy

Cairn is a personal project with one user: its author. This page describes exactly what the app touches and where it goes, in plain language.

The short version

  • Your notes and reminders are stored in your own Google Cloud Firestore database.
  • Images and recordings are stored in your own Google Drive.
  • Your email is read on your own device and is never sent to any server the developer runs.
  • The developer operates no server and has no copy of your data.

What Cairn reads, and why

Your name, email address and profile picture
To sign you in and to label which account is connected.
Gmail (read-only)
To find deadlines — assignment due dates in Google Classroom notification emails, and dates mentioned by teachers. Message content is read on your device. Only the result, such as “a reminder due Friday”, is saved. Message bodies are never stored and never transmitted to the developer.
Google Classroom (read-only)
To read your course list, assignments and their due dates, announcements, and whether you have submitted an assignment — so a reminder can tick itself off when you hand work in.
Google Drive (files created by Cairn only)
To store images and audio you attach to a note. Cairn uses the drive.file scope, which means it can only see files it created itself. It cannot read the rest of your Drive.

Where your data lives

Notes, reminders and their metadata are stored in Cloud Firestore, in a Google Cloud project belonging to the user. Attachments are stored in the signed-in user's own Google Drive and count against that account's storage. Access is restricted by security rules so that a signed-in account can read and write only its own data.

If you connect more than one Google account — for example a personal account and a university account — only the first is used to store data. Any additional account is used solely to read its own Gmail and Classroom, and nothing is written to it.

Automatic sorting of email

Cairn can sort incoming mail into important and unimportant. Most of this is decided on your device by fixed rules, with no third party involved.

If, and only if, you supply your own Google Gemini API key, mail that the rules cannot classify is sent to Google's Gemini API for scoring. What is sent is the sender, subject line, a short snippet and the date — not the full message. This is a choice you make: the feature is off until you add a key, and Cairn works without one. Your use of that API is governed by Google's terms for it.

What is not collected

  • No analytics, telemetry or usage tracking.
  • No advertising, and no sharing or selling of data to anyone.
  • No account on any service operated by the developer, because there isn't one.

Removing access

You can disconnect any account from inside Cairn, which deletes the stored credentials for it. You can also revoke Cairn's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which works whether or not the app is installed. Deleting the Firestore database removes stored notes and reminders; deleting the files from your Drive removes attachments.

Limited Use

Cairn's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
